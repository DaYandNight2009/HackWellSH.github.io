About HackWell 2026

HackWell is the first ever Wellington hackathon competition across the Wellington college schools in China, completely student-led by the pupils in Wellington College International Shanghai. We are hoping that this event will continue prospering and developing in the future, and continue to be a platform for students to learn and develop their skills in the field of computer science and technology.

In previous years, our hackathon functioned as an internal initiative, where we worked with students designing the prompts that guided the competition. This year marks a significant development, as Geely Auto Group has joined as our headline sponsor, setting the stage for a transformative event. Furthermore, experts from Geely will conduct workshops during the event, offering participants valuable professional insights. The hackathon has thus evolved beyond a computer science competition and has been transformed into a comprehensive STEM event, fostering interdisciplinary learning and collaboration.

We welcome everyone who is willing to join our HackWell and are looking forward to seeing you at the competition! Parents are welcome to join on the final day to attend the projects showcase and closing ceremony.

If you wish to sign up, please contact your Computer Science/ICT teacher.

- HackWell Organizing Team
