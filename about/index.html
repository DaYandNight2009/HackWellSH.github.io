ABOUT HACKWELL

A student-led STEM event for the Wellington community, built around curiosity, collaboration and the confidence to try.

THE STORY

Beyond a computer science competition.

HackWell is the first ever Wellington hackathon competition across the Wellington College schools in China, completely student-led by the pupils in Wellington College International Shanghai.

In previous years, the hackathon functioned as an internal initiative. This year, Geely Auto Group has joined as headline sponsor, with experts conducting workshops and offering professional insights. The event has grown into a comprehensive STEM experience.

Parents are welcome on the final day for the project showcase and closing ceremony.
