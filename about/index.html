THE STORY

Beyond a computer science competition.

HackWell is Wellington's hackathon competition across the Wellington College schools in China, completely student-led by the pupils in Wellington College International Shanghai.

In previous years, the hackathon functioned as an internal initiative. This year marks the second year Geely Auto Group joins us as our headline sponsor, and we have also invited experts to conduct workshops and offer professional insights. We hope this event will be an enriching STEM experience that prompts students to shape their perceptions of technology in our modern world.

Parents are welcome on the final day for the project showcase and closing ceremony.

“Beyond the Screen” represents a critical ethical blind spot in modern software engineering: the disconnect between what a developer builds on their display and how that code impacts user privacy and data protection in the physical world. While developers often focus entirely on functionality, code optimization, and interface design within the controlled environment of their monitors, the real-world deployment of ambient tech, spatial computing, and IoT devices routinely violates the physical boundaries of human lives.
