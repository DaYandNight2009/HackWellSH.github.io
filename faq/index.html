05 / FIELD NOTES

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Answering the important questions before you step beyond the screen.

What is a hackathon?

Hackathons are in-person coding events where teenagers come together to learn new skills, create fun projects and make memories. Instead of hacking bank accounts, you'll build something meaningful to you.

Who can participate?

All students from Year 7 to Year 13 are invited to participate in HackWell for free.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. This is a STEM hackathon, including various elements of STEM, not just computer science.

What do I need to bring?

Your Computer Science or ICT teacher will have a packing list for the days.

What will be provided?

Lunch and dinner will be provided on Saturday, and lunch only will be provided on Sunday. Snacks will also be provided.

How do the groups work?

Groups of four will be announced near the date of the event.
